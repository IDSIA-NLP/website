We are the natural language processing (NLP) group of [IDSIA](https://www.idsia.ch), the Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (USI-SUPSI) in Lugano, Switzerland. We combine basic research, mostly funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation, with applied projects together with hospitals and companies.
