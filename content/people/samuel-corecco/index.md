Samuel is interested in generative AI, from large language models to image generation, and in AI applications in science.

He holds a bachelor's degree in computer science and a master's degree in artificial intelligence from Università della Svizzera italiana (USI). His master's thesis applied deep learning to the analysis of solar spectral lines.
