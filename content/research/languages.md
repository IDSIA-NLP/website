Language models work best for a handful of large languages. We study why, starting from the text itself: how words are split into pieces (tokenization), how they are represented inside multilingual models, and how specific scripts impact the distribution of information in text data in different languages.

The goal is language technology that serves speakers of all languages and dialects more equally. Our work on tokenization bias in dialects received a Senior Area Chair award at EMNLP 2025.
