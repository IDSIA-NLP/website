theme: languages

summary: "How language models deal with dialects, scripts and less-resourced languages, and how to make them fairer across languages."
---
Language models work best for a handful of large languages. We study why, starting from the text itself: how words are split into pieces (tokenization), how scripts and dialects are represented inside models, and how much information text carries in different languages.

The goal is language technology that serves speakers of all languages and dialects more equally. Our work on tokenization bias in dialects received a Senior Area Chair award at EMNLP 2025.
