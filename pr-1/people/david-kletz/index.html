David Kletz

Postdoctoral researcher (former member, 2025–2026)

  • Understanding and trusting language models
  • Languages, dialects and multilinguality

ORCIDGoogle Scholar

David worked on interpretability of language models and on using them for information retrieval in specialised documents. He holds a PhD in NLP from Sorbonne Nouvelle and Université Paris Cité, on how pre-trained language models represent negation.
