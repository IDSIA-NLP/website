Projects

Research projects funded by public agencies, and applied projects with companies and hospitals. Some industry work is confidential and not listed.

Current

TaxoMine

Mining taxonomic knowledge from the scientific literature

Continues our work with biologists on extracting information about species from published papers, organised by taxonomy.

Research 2026–now · Swiss National Science Foundation (SNF)

AutoDischarge

Semi-automated generation of discharge summaries

Helps doctors write discharge letters faster by drafting them from the patient record, for the doctor to check and complete.

Applied 2025–now · Innosuisse

M2P2

Medical, multilingual and privacy-preserving NLP in the clinical domain

Makes modern language technology usable in Swiss hospitals: across the country's languages and without exposing patient data.

Research 2025–now · Swiss National Science Foundation (SNF)

Past

QUADRATIC

NLP in support of pharmacovigilance: Quality Adverse Drug Reaction Active Control

Finds reports of adverse drug reactions in clinical text, to support drug-safety monitoring.

Research 2024–2025 · Swiss National Science Foundation (SNF)

Swiss AI Initiative

Coordination of IDSIA's participation in the Swiss AI Initiative

Coordinated IDSIA's part in the national effort to build and apply large AI models on the Alps supercomputer at CSCS.

Research 2024

Brisk.AI

Simplified and translated scientific literature for education

Used AI to produce simplified and translated versions of scientific papers for teaching.

Research 2023–2024

DAG-MTSM

Detecting misinformation and machine-generated text

Developed and tested ways to detect misleading content and text written by language models. Produced the BUST benchmark.

Research 2023

ELDI

Dialect identification in short texts

Developed methods to recognise the dialect of short texts such as social media posts, focusing on varieties of Arabic.

Research 2023

Mini-MUSE

AI-based visualisation methods to explore digitised publications

Built interactive visual tools to explore large collections of digitised historical publications.

Research 2023–2024

PREGAMUS

Reducing the complexity of scientific literature

Used AI to make scientific papers easier to read, as a support against misinformation.

Research 2023

WRSD

Identifying the risk of, and preventing, work-related stress disorders

Explored how text and data can help identify the risk of work-related stress.

Applied 2022–2024

ArthroTraitMine

Mining arthropod traits from the literature

Built text-mining workflows and a database of traits of arthropods (insects, spiders, crustaceans and relatives) collected from the scientific literature.

Research 2021–2023

BERGAMOS

Open, machine-readable annotations of biomedical literature

Connected our entity recognition tool to PubAnnotation, so researchers can automatically annotate collections of biomedical papers.

Research 2021–2022 · SERI bridging grant

INCdid

Dialect identification in noisy text

Developed methods to identify dialects in short, noisy texts, especially between closely related language varieties.

Research 2021–2022

MisInfoCOV

Misinformation in social media during COVID-19

Studied how to identify misinformation on social media, and public stance towards health policies, during the pandemic.

Research 2021–2022

COVID-19 Twitter Monitor

Social media mining for health

Combined ten methods for analysing COVID-19 tweets into one public platform with interactive visualisations.

Research 2020–2021

LifeLike / BOOST

Voice-based training simulations

With SkillGym, moved a leadership-training simulator from clicking options to speaking, using language understanding to follow the conversation.

Applied 2020–2021 · Industry

TalentScout

Entity recognition for CVs and job ads

With a pharmaceutical company, built entity recognition for job titles, organisations and places in CVs, outperforming off-the-shelf models.

Applied 2020 · Industry

Patient insights from social media

Mining patient insights in social media conversations

With Roche, extracted information from patient conversations online to support patient-centred drug development.

Applied 2019–2021 · Industry

StageAI

Conversational recommendation of performers

With Stagend, improved a platform that matches performers to events, using multilingual question answering to fill in missing information.

Applied 2019–2020 · Industry

SwissMADE

Automated detection of adverse drug events from older inpatients' records

Used NLP and data mining on electronic medical records from five Swiss hospitals to detect adverse drug events in older patients. Part of the National Research Programme NRP 74 'Smarter Health Care'.

Research 2017–2021 · Swiss National Science Foundation (NRP 74)
