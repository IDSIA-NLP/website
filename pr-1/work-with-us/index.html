Work with us

We welcome collaborations with companies, hospitals and other research groups, as well as students and new team members.

Companies and organisations

Many of our projects start from a concrete problem brought by a company or a hospital. Typical collaborations are:

  • Innosuisse projects: applied research co-funded by the Swiss Innovation Agency, with a company as implementation partner.
  • Direct contracts: shorter studies, prototypes or assessments of language technology for a specific need.

If you have text data and a question about it, get in touch.

Students

We supervise bachelor’s and master’s theses and internships, mainly for students at USI and SUPSI. Topics follow our research themes. Write to us with a short CV and the topics that interest you.

Open positions

Open positions are announced in News. Unsolicited applications are welcome.
