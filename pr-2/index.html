Research Projects People Publications Resources News Work with us

We study how computers understand and produce human language, and how to make language technology reliable and useful in practice.

We are the natural language processing (NLP) group of IDSIA, the Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (USI-SUPSI) in Lugano, Switzerland. We combine basic research, mostly funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation, with applied projects together with hospitals and companies.

Research themes

All research →

Health and clinical text

Language technology for hospitals and health research: clinical notes, drug safety and patient privacy.

Languages, dialects and multilinguality

How language models deal with dialects, scripts and less-resourced languages, and how to make them fairer across languages.

Understanding and trusting language models

When language models get things wrong, and how to tell: hallucinations, machine-generated text, uncertainty.

Knowledge from scientific literature

Turning scientific papers into structured, searchable knowledge, and making science easier to read.

Current projects

All projects →
  • BM-TOK How to build the vocabulary of a multilingual language model so that less-resourced languages and non-Latin scripts are represented fairly.
  • AutoDischarge Helps doctors write discharge letters faster by drafting them from the patient record, for the doctor to check and complete.
  • UBS–IDSIA AI Lab The NLP group participates in the collaboration across different NLP-related projects.
  • GURU (ENERGENIUS) A conversational interface where citizens ask in natural language about energy savings, costs and regulation.
